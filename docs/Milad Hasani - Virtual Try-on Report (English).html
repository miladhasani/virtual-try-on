Virtual Try-On Models

Milad Hasani

I built Virtual Try-On on my own laptop, an RTX 4070 with eight gigs. I wanted to say why each option is there, which clothes it handles better, and what this card can actually do. No retraining — just running the models. Seven weights load here. Gemini and the Hugging Face Space are for when the GPU is busy or too small for FLUX: the same person and garment photos go out, and the heavy weights never sit on the laptop.

How I put it together

What a checkpoint is The weight file — the numbers the network learned. The paper says weights; Hugging Face says checkpoint. There are nine options in the menu, but not nine separate networks. Mix, VITON-HD, and DressCode share one UNet and only swap the attention file — about 198 MB. Mask-Free is different: an 8-channel Pix2Pix UNet. The three FLUX options bring Fill or LoRA weights. Gemini and the Space load nothing locally.

Input is always simple: a photo of a person, a photo of a garment, and whether I want the top, the bottom, or the full look. I center-crop the person onto a 3:4 canvas and letterbox the garment with a white border to the same size. The paper uses DensePose and SCHP for the mask; I used SegFormer so it would start on Windows without Detectron2. Then one of four paths runs: SD inpaint, Pix2Pix, FLUX, or the cloud. If something breaks, it no longer stays in the terminal — a red bar at the top shows the same text. Generate locks while a job is running, and Stop only lights up when something is actually in flight.

SD 1.5 inpaint — three attention weights

One shared UNet, about eight gigs. Switching models only moves that 198 MB of attention. I set guidance to 2.5. No Hugging Face login.

FLUX and cloud

FLUX.1-Fill-dev is gated and its guidance is 30. On a small card it goes Offload or 4-bit, but slowly. Gemini with an AI Studio key and the public CatVTON Space on ZeroGPU load no weights on the laptop.

FLUX · Alpha · Beta · Gemini · HF Space

Quality comes from the menu. Tiny is 256×384 and 16 steps, Fast 384×512 and 20 steps, Balanced — what I use day to day — 576×768 and 30 steps. Quality is 768×1024 and 50 steps, Studio 1024×1280 and 36 steps. If VRAM is Auto and the card is under 24 GB, FLUX switches itself to Offload or 4-bit.

What these four numbers mean

The paper reports four scores. The first two compare a pair of photos: my output next to a real photo of the same person in the same clothes. The other two compare the whole set of outputs to the whole set of real photos, even when there is no exact pair.

MetricWhat it saysBetterRangeWhere it lies
SSIMWindow by window, how close it is to the real photoHigher, near 10 to 1A blurry but aligned image can beat a sharp one that is slightly shifted
LPIPSHow much difference a network “eye” feelsLowerUsually 0.04 to 0.20Depends on that network; it does not score the face on its own
FIDHow close the whole set of outputs is to real photosLowerUnitless; here about 3 to 15The number jumps if the eval code or the sample count changes
KIDSame idea, calmer on a small setLowerUnitlessStill lives in Inception space

SSIM

It compares window by window with the real photo: brightness, contrast, edges. The score sits between zero and one. For me a high SSIM means the face, hair, and background stayed put — not that the garment looks nicer. In the paper table, CatVTON inpaint on VITON-HD reaches 0.8704. Close, not identical.

LPIPS

Not raw pixels. Patches go through a network like VGG and the distance is measured there. The eye cares more about texture and folds than an RGB gap; this approximates that. Lower is better. If the garment sits right but the color is a bit off, LPIPS complains more than SSIM. Inpaint on VITON-HD is 0.0565.

FID

This one is not for a single pair. All the real photos and all the outputs go through Inception, each pile gets a mean, and you see how far the two clouds sit. Low FID means the outputs look like real photos. The catch is that changing the eval code or the number of images moves the number. That is why I do not mix the community FLUX FIDs into the paper table.

KID

Same space, without FID’s statistical assumption. On a few thousand images it usually sits still. The paper reports both because everyone knows FID and KID jumps less on these sets. On paired VITON-HD, CatVTON hits 0.411 and OOTDiffusion 4.086.

What I do before the model

All nine start from the same person, garment, and garment type. From here their paths split.

StepWhat it doesWhere in the code
1. CanvasI center-crop the person; I pad the garment with a white border to the same sizeresize_and_crop / padding
2. Body parseSegFormer splits clothes into 18 parts: face, upper clothes, trousers, hands. It stays on CPU so the GPU stays freeClothMasker
3. MaskFor Mix and the other inpaints, the region grows with Top / Bottom / Full look and the face and hair stay. Mask-Free does not send this to the UNetbuild_agnostic_mask
4. EdgeI blur the inpaint mask a little so the garment edge does not tearblur_mask
5. GenerateSD inpaint, Pix2Pix, FLUX, Gemini, or the Hugging Face Spacecatvton / flux_tryon / cloud
6. LockOn Mask-Free Top I restore trousers, legs, shoes, and the face from the original photocomposite_tryon

The paper has DensePose and SCHP. I used SegFormer because Windows install was simpler. Mask-Free is for when the mask catches on belts and busy prints — with the real Pix2Pix UNet, not the 9-channel inpaint one.

Why I put these in

Mix — what I run every day

Day to day I go to Mix. It is the same SD inpaint; the difference is that its attention learned on about 48 thousand mixed pairs at 1024 pixels, tops and trousers both. On my laptop it stays stable at Balanced and needs no login. Print and texture still trail FLUX.

id catvton-mix · zhengchong/CatVTON · mix-48k-1024 · Guidance 2.5

VITON-HD — shirts and jackets

Same engine as Mix; only the attention file changes. It learned on shirts and jackets. I keep it for upper-body catalogs. I do not reach for it if I want trousers or a full outfit.

id catvton-vitonhd · vitonhd-16k-512

DressCode — dresses and variety

I pick this when I want an evening dress or a full set. That paired FID of 3.992 in the paper belongs to this family on DressCode, not to Mix.

id catvton-dresscode · dresscode-16k-512

Mask-Free — busy photos

This one does not give the network a mask. Person and garment sit side by side and the model guesses where the clothes should change. At first I loaded the inpaint UNet by mistake and it stripped the clothes instead of putting them on. Now it is the right Pix2Pix. The next problem was that with Top it sometimes changed the trousers too, so after generation I restore trousers, legs, shoes, and the face from the original photo. On a clean photo it is not better than Mix.

id catvton-maskfree · zhengchong/CatVTON-MaskFree · timbrooks/instruct-pix2pix

Gemini — Google cloud

No local weights. Person and garment go out and a try-on photo comes back. I put the key from aistudio.google.com/apikey in the UI field; it stays on this machine in .secrets.json and never goes to git. I try gemini-3.1-flash-image first, then gemini-2.5-flash-image if that model is missing. I pick this when the GPU is busy or too small for FLUX. Without a key, the error banner shows the same AI Studio link.

id gemini-flash · Google AI Studio key · no local VRAM

Hugging Face — public CatVTON Space

Same cloud idea, but the engine is CatVTON, not Gemini. The authors’ Space zhengchong/CatVTON is often RUNTIME_ERROR. I check what is live and go to tryitonvirtual/virtual-tryon or Nandha2017/virtual-tryon — both on ZeroGPU A10G. Without a token the quota is thin; one run asked for 180 seconds and only 176 were left. An hf_ token from huggingface.co/settings/tokens raises the quota and also unlocks gated FLUX downloads.

id hf-catvton · ZeroGPU · optional token

Authors’ FLUX — light and folds

The CatVTON authors’ own LoRA on FluxFill, about 37 MB. I set guidance to 30. Light and folds beat SD; on eight gigs it only moves with Offload or 4-bit, and it is slow.

id catvton-flux · Guidance 30 · concat person_first

FLUX Alpha — logos and texture

A community LoRA on VITON-HD. I pick this when logos and text on the fabric matter. They report an FID of 6.068; I do not mix it into the paper table.

id catvton-flux-alpha · xiaozaa/catvton-flux-lora-alpha

FLUX Beta — garment variety

It swaps the whole transformer, about 23.8 GB. More garment variety than Alpha, and the heaviest download.

The hard parts

Windows and weight downloads Hugging Face’s XET layer kept stalling on Windows. I turned XET off and put a resume-capable curl script in. The SD 1.5 UNet itself is gated too; I try a public mirror first.
Eight gigs versus FLUX My card is 8 GB. SD 1.5 fits at Balanced. Native FLUX wants 24 GB. Offload and 4-bit make it run, but very slowly. That is why I also put Gemini and the Hugging Face Space in, so the heavy weights never sit on this laptop.
Mask-Free without a trousers lock Pix2Pix has no inpaint mask. On Top, it once changed the subject’s trousers too. After generation I restore trousers, legs, shoes, and the face from the original photo. If the parser misses the trousers, the lock is incomplete.
ZeroGPU quota and the Gemini key The Space is live and the queue and upload work, but without an hf_ token the guest quota is thin. Gemini does not run at all without a key; the error text has to show at the top, not only in the terminal. I keep the key and token in .secrets.json.
A simpler mask than the paper The paper has DensePose and SCHP. I put SegFormer on the CPU so the GPU stays free for generation. Install got easier; the mask edge can be weaker.
Non-commercial licenses CatVTON and SD weights are CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. FLUX.1 [dev] is also non-commercial and gated. Gemini follows Google’s Gemini API terms and takes the photos off the machine. If I ever want commercial work, I need a different model or a separate license.

Last word

If I only look at the paper numbers, CatVTON inpaint is both light and accurate: paired FID 5.425 on VITON-HD and 3.992 on DressCode, about 4 GB and 9 seconds at 1024 on their card. Mask-Free sits a little behind, but it does not depend on a clean mask.

I put that family on the laptop. With Mix, Fast is about 11 seconds and Balanced about 19. FLUX raises the ceiling, but it also brings login, size, and VRAM. Gemini and the Space are for when the GPU is busy or FLUX is not worth it on this card. Day to day, Mix is enough. I pick Mask-Free when the mask is torn. I pick FLUX or Gemini when fabric detail or a VRAM shortage actually pays for it.
